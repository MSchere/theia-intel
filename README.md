# Theia Intel: Illuminating the Shadows of Conflict

## About Theia Intel

Theia Intel is a comprehensive defense-oriented OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence) dashboard that illuminates the complex world of global conflicts. Our platform integrates defense intelligence, global news, and financial data to provide unparalleled insights for defense professionals, analysts, and decision-makers.

## Core Features

### 1. Defense and Conflict OSINT

-   Real-time war statistics and battle updates
-   Military personnel tracking in main battlefronts
-   Significant event monitoring (attacks, bombings, terrorism)
-   In-depth geopolitical tension analysis

### 2. Global Intelligence

-   Curated defense-related political and economic news
-   International relations and diplomatic developments tracker
-   Arms trade and military technology advancement monitoring

### 3. Financial Integration

-   Defense industry market data and interactive charts
-   Correlation analysis between conflict events and market activities
-   Economic impact assessment of global conflicts

### 4. Specialized Tools

-   Customizable reporting and advanced data visualization
-   Interactive timeline creation for evolving conflicts
-   Risk assessment models for conflict-prone regions

## Who It's For
